Where those results came from

Five reasons your brand is stuck at ₹10L

We fix them in this order: RTO first, then product, then scale.

Orders come back unpaid

A quarter to half of your COD orders come back. You pay shipping twice, and the revenue on Shopify never fully reaches your bank.

One product carries everything

Every time a winning product dies, it takes weeks to find the next one. Until then, the same one winner keeps rotating and growth stalls with it.

Ads wear out every week

Two or three ads carry the whole account. ROAS slips a little every week and nothing new is ready to replace them.

Burned by the last agency

Months of 'testing', reports instead of revenue, and an agency you had to chase. Now every pitch sounds the same.

You're running all of it

Ads at night, designers on WhatsApp, the courier on hold. The brand only moves as fast as your evenings.

What does it cost?

A monthly retainer that depends on your ad spend and how much creative you need. We'll give you the exact number on the call once we've seen your account. Quoting before that would be guessing.

How fast will we see results?

Ads go live within 8 days. Restructuring usually shows up within two to three weeks. Real revenue change normally lands in month two or three, because the RTO, product and creative work build on each other rather than switching on. Anyone promising month-one results is either lucky or lying.

Do you guarantee ROAS or revenue?

No. Nobody honest can. What we commit to is the order of work (RTO first, then product, then scale), a 3-month projection, a weekly call, and your numbers live on your phone. You'll always know what's being done and why.

Will you increase our ad budget?

Often the opposite at first. Some of our best results came from cutting spend and removing campaigns competing with each other. We scale budget when the numbers justify it, not to justify our fee.

What do you need from me?

Access to your ad account, Shopify and courier panel, a weekly call, and someone who can make product decisions with us. You keep dispatch. The rest is on us.

What happens to my accounts if we stop?

Your ad accounts, your data and your dashboard stay yours. If we stop working together, everything we built stays with you, and our access is removed within seven days.
